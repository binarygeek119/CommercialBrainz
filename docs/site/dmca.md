# DMCA takedown policy

Submit a DMCA takedown request to hide a YouTube link from public view. Metadata is preserved for archival purposes.

**Contact:** [commercialbrainz@outlook.com](mailto:commercialbrainz@outlook.com)

## What we remove

When a valid notice is accepted, the YouTube link is hidden from public pages. Catalog metadata (titles, brands, dates, and so on) stays in the archive so the historical record is not wiped.

## How to file

Use the form on this page. You will need:

- The **video CBID** (UUID in the video or commercial URL)
- Your name, email, and a description of the copyrighted work and infringement
- An electronic signature (your full legal name)

Notices are reviewed by the moderation team. Incomplete or abusive filings may be rejected.
