# How to become a moderator

Moderators help keep CommercialBrainz accurate and safe. There is **no automatic promotion** — an admin grants the **mod** role after you show reliable judgment on the site.

## What mods can do

Mods (and admins) typically:

- **Auto-apply or reject** community edits without waiting for the full vote window
- Review the **DMCA** queue and content **reports**
- Work **dead links**, deletion requests, and the **fingerprint** / hashing queue
- Use the on-site **Mod** tools (nav link appears once you have the role)

Mods are also treated as **auto-editors** for voting eligibility and related checks.

## Path to mod

1. **Use the site well**
   - [Register](/register), verify email, and stay active.
   - Unlock [Submit](/submit/upgrade), then add careful metadata and links.
   - [Vote](/voting) thoughtfully on open edits.

2. **Build a track record**
   - Accepted edits and helpful votes raise **reputation**.
   - Prefer quality over speed: correct brands, years, master vs sub links, clear notes for voters.
   - Avoid spammy bulk submissions until you understand [Terms](/terms) and (if relevant) Power User / bulk rules.

3. **Help where mods already look**
   - Flag bad content with report tools when available.
   - Fix obvious metadata yourself via edits instead of only complaining.
   - Join [Discord](https://discord.gg/AEhVjqX4Af) and be constructive.

4. **Ask an admin**
   - After you have a visible history, ask a current **admin** (or mod who can loop one in) on Discord or by email: [commercialbrainz@outlook.com](mailto:commercialbrainz@outlook.com).
   - Say who you are on the site, what you’ve been working on, and why you want the role.
   - Admins set roles in the Admin panel (`POST`-style role change on the user). There is no self-serve “apply to be mod” button.

## What we look for

- Consistent, careful edits and votes
- Willingness to handle boring queue work (reports, DMCA, dead links)
- Calm communication; no sockpuppets or vote brigades
- Comfort reading [Terms](/terms) and explaining them to newcomers

We may decline or wait if the community is small, queues are light, or we need more time to see your work.

## After you are promoted

- The **Mod** link appears in the nav.
- Prefer applying clear consensus edits; leave controversial ones for community vote when that helps legitimacy.
- Escalate security issues privately ([SECURITY.md](https://github.com/binarygeek119/CommercialBrainz/blob/testing/.github/SECURITY.md)), not in public issues.
- You can still [donate](/donate) time — moderation *is* donating time.
